Our Work & Programs

How we translate maternal love into daily care, clinical support, and special education for our children.

1. Residential Care

A Secure & Loving Ghar

We offer safe, hygienic, and family-like 24/7 residential care. Unlike cold clinical institutions, Sarlaben's home provides round-the-clock supervised living quarters where caregivers ensure safety, warmth, clean beds, and unconditional love.

  • 24/7 Caregiver Monitoring
  • Clean, Ventilated Living Quarters
  • Warm Family Atmosphere
2. Special Education & Skills

Dignified Padhai & Tuition

Intellectual and daily skill development is crucial for children with intellectual disabilities. Our special educators conduct individualized cognitive sessions, providing school tuition, books, and pre-vocational skills to help kids perform simple daily tasks independently.

  • Individualized Cognitive Lessons
  • Daily Self-Care Skill Training (hygiene, dressing)
  • Basic Stationery, Craft & Book Supply
3. Food & Medical Monitoring

Hygienic Meals & Medical Seva

Our in-house kitchen operates under a strict wholesome diet policy where fresh meals are prepared daily. Residents undergo regular health screenings, psychiatric assessments, and pharmaceutical care overseen by Dr. Nipa Mendapara (MD, Pharmacology) and our healthcare team.

  • 100% In-House Kitchen Cooked Wholesome Meals
  • Psychiatric Screening & Medicine Management
  • Regular Physical Health Checkups
4. Healing Therapies

Creative Art, Music & Yoga

We heal through expression. Music, dance, and drawing therapy are scheduled regularly to improve emotional expression, motor coordination, and sensory integration, while daily yoga sessions maintain physical agility.

  • Music & Dance Therapy Sessions
  • Creative Painting & Handicraft Outlets
  • Gentle Yoga & Motor Agility Exercises
5. Social Integration

Picnics, Outings & Festivals

Mental challenges should not mean isolation. Our children celebrate major festivals, birthdays, and go on regular picnics. We organize community integration trips, including our memorable group pilgrimage to Prayagraj.

  • Group Outings & Nature Picnics
  • Grand Festive Celebrations (Diwali, Ganesh Utsav)
  • Inter-school & Community Interaction Events
LIVE PROJECT • RURAL RAJKOT
6. Current Priority Initiative

Rural School & Sanctuary Construction

We are actively constructing a dedicated, purpose-built school building in rural Gujarat for children with intellectual disabilities. This new permanent facility will feature specialized classrooms, sensory therapy spaces, safe playgrounds, and vocational workshops to secure an independent and dignified future for our children.

  • Dedicated Specialized Classrooms & Sensory Rooms
  • Safe, Barrier-Free Accessible Campus Architecture
  • Vocational Workshops for Practical Independence
