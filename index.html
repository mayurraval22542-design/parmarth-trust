OUR SERVICE & SEVA

About Parmarth Trust

Parmarth Education & Charitable Trust is an ISO Certified, government-registered NGO based in Rajkot, Gujarat. Built upon the foundation of over 35+ years of personal, unconditional dedication to mentally challenged children (Divyang), our trust works tirelessly to bring hope, care, and dignity to special souls.

At Parmarth, we believe that intellectual challenges should not mean isolation. We provide a complete rehabilitation ecosystem that replaces cold clinical institutions with a nurturing, supervised home sanctuary. We currently provide residential care, medical monitoring, and therapy for 17 children, including 8 full-time permanent residents.

Our services are structured around three fundamental care concepts: **providing clean, safe residential housing (ghar)**, **providing custom specialized education & cognitive sessions (padhana)**, and **delivering dedicated care & therapies (seva)**. To ensure maximum support to parents and families, all care is provided 100% free of cost.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To establish a world-class rehabilitation environment in Rajkot, Gujarat, where no child is left behind in isolation. We focus on transitioning special children from neglect to high-quality care.

  • Specialized clinical care & 24/7 psychiatric monitoring.
  • Holistic mental, physical, and emotional development.
  • 100% free residential shelter and training for families.

Our Vision

To become a beacon of hope and dignity in Gujarat for individuals with intellectual disabilities. We envision a compassionate society that treats every special soul as an equal, valuable member of the community.

  • Profound respect, equality, and opportunity for all.
  • Complete eradication of social isolation for special children.
  • Sustainable, lifelong residential family sanctuaries.

Our Complete Care System

Our rehabilitation programs ensure holistic well-being for our children, completely free of charge.

1. Safe Residential Care (24/7 Supervised)

Providing secure, hygienic, and family-like residence at our Rajkot campus. Round-the-clock supervised living quarters with dedicated caregivers ensuring safety, comfort, and unconditional love.

2. Intellectual & Daily Skill Development

Cognitive development sessions tailored to different intellectual capacities. Focus on teaching daily living skills (self-care, personal hygiene, communication) to foster maximal independent living.

3. Specialized Medical Monitoring

Regular physical and mental health screenings by qualified professionals. Expert-supervised psychiatric monitoring, pharmaceutical management, and on-site clinical care.

4. Healing Through Art & Therapy

Music, dance, and art therapy to improve emotional expression, motor skills, and sensory integration. Structured art sessions allowing creative outlets and focus.

5. Parent & Family Counseling

Guidance programs and psychological support for families to help them understand and support their children constructively.

6. Social Integration & Outings

Promoting self-confidence through community interaction, group celebrations, festivals, picnics, and educational outings. Major trips such as our recent group pilgrimage to Prayagraj.

7. Vocational & Pre-Vocational Training

Pre-vocational skills program designed to build confidence and train residents in performing simple, meaningful tasks.

8. Rural School & Sanctuary Construction (Active Priority)

Constructing a permanent, specialized school campus with therapy rooms, accessible classrooms, and vocational workshops for special children in rural Gujarat.
