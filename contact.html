GET INVOLVED

Ways You Can Help

Explore how you can align with our mission and empower children with special needs.

Sponsor a Child

Sponsor a child's complete monthly rehabilitation costs, which cover 24/7 residential shelter, clinical therapies, food, psychiatric care, and schooling.

Sponsor Meals

Provide nutritious meals prepared in our own kitchen. You can choose to sponsor a single wholesome meal (lunch or dinner) or support full-day nutrition for all our children.

Become a Volunteer

Offer your time, skill, or presence. Volunteer as a special tutor, assist caregivers during outings, celebrate milestones with our children, or sponsor clothing.
