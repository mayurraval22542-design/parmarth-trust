# Parmarth Education & Charitable Trust

> **A Home Built on Love, A Future Built on Hope.**

Official website of **Parmarth Education & Charitable Trust** (Reg No. E-7740), a government-registered NGO in Rajkot, Gujarat, providing 100% free lifelong residential care, medical monitoring, therapy, and cognitive rehabilitation for children with intellectual disabilities.

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/mayurraval22542-design/parmarth-trust)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Responsive & Modern UI**: Built with a signature Deep Forest Green (`#062416`) & Gold (`#dca134`) palette.
- **100% Mobile Ready**: Audited and tested across smartphones (320px–480px), tablets (768px), and desktops.
- **Fundraising & Donation Integration**: Direct UPI QR, Google Pay, SBI Bank Details accordion, and 80G tax exemption clarity.
- **Urgent School Project Popup**: Live status initiative for rural special school building construction.
- **Interactive Multi-Page Structure**:
  - `index.html` — Homepage with Hero, Impact Metrics, Founder Story, Carousel, and QR Donation Card.
  - `about.html` — Mission, Vision, Core Values, 4-Member Dedicated Team, and Government Registrations.
  - `programs.html` — 6 Holistic Programs & Priority Initiative #6 with Dynamic Photo Gallery.
  - `contact.html` — Sanctuary Location, Google Map, Visiting Hours, and Volunteer / Inquiry Form.
