Our Story & Heritage

The heart, philosophy, and history of dedication behind the Parmarth Education & Charitable Trust.

HOW IT BEGAN

A Journey of Unconditional Love

Built on over 40+ years of personal mission work by Sarlaben Trivedi.

Sarlaben Trivedi has spent a lifetime advocating for mentally challenged children who were often neglected or misunderstood by society. Recognizing that clinical institutions cannot replace the warmth of a family, she made a monumental decision 5 years ago: she opened the doors of her own residential home to serve as a permanent, safe shelter.

Officially registered in 2011, Parmarth Trust in Rajkot, Gujarat, is today a registered NGO supporting 17 children, with 8 full-time permanent residents living under Sarlaben's roof. We provide complete pediatric, psychiatric, and cognitive rehabilitation, all 100% free of cost to their families.

“

Every smile we restore is a testament to the power of a loving family environment. We are not a clinical institution; we are their lifelong home.”

— Sarlaben Trivedi Founder & Director, Parmarth Trust

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To establish a world-class rehabilitation environment in Rajkot, Gujarat, where no child is left behind in isolation. We focus on transitioning special children from neglect to high-quality care.

  • Specialized clinical care & 24/7 psychiatric monitoring.
  • Holistic mental, physical, and emotional development.
  • 100% free residential shelter and training for families.

Our Vision

To become a beacon of hope and dignity in Gujarat for individuals with intellectual disabilities. We envision a compassionate society that treats every special soul as an equal, valuable member of the community.

  • Profound respect, equality, and opportunity for all.
  • Complete eradication of social isolation for special children.
  • Sustainable, lifelong residential family sanctuaries.
WHAT DRIVES US

Our Core Values

The pillars of our daily service, ensuring that our residential sanctuary is a place of joy and dignity.

Unconditional Seva

Treating every special child with pure maternal affection, ensuring their comfort, clinical needs, and emotional happiness are fully met every single day.

Holistic Padhai

Providing cognitive exercises, tuition, and daily skills training to develop self-care and maximize independent living and confidence.

Secure Ghar

A safe, hygienic, ISO-certified residential sanctuary in Rajkot under 24/7 caregiver supervision, creating a nurturing and clean living space.

Family Atmosphere

Shunning institutional coldness. We celebrate festivals, birthdays, share meals, and go on outings together as one big, happy family.
